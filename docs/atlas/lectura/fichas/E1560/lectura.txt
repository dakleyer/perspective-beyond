# Supply Chain Rationalization & Process Mining

A global luxury group needed to understand and rationalize technology across highly complex supply chain environments.

In luxury, supply chain complexity is not only about moving products from factory to boutique. It includes small production series, thousands of components, long repair lifecycles, high‑value spare parts, multi‑country movements, fiscal and compliance‑driven flows, many legal entities and very high customer expectations.

Loosely speaking, logistics is not “mission critical” in the petrochemical sense, but it is reputation‑critical: service failures, unclear repair timelines or repeated delays directly affect brand trust and perceived value.

In this context, supply chain intelligence is not just an efficiency lever. It becomes part of the promise made to the client when they buy or service a high‑value piece.
