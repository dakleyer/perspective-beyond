# Piece Identity & Inventory Orchestration

Connecting anti-counterfeiting, RFID, digital fingerprints and high-value piece movement.

In high-end luxury, managing physical pieces is not a simple inventory problem. It is a problem of trust, authenticity, logistics, client experience and operational choreography.

Thousands of references in production, tens of thousands of pieces circulating, and decades of historical production including rare and highly valuable pieces.

Pieces move across boutiques, vaults, repair centers, private events, rooms, trays and presentations, often under strict timing and scarcity constraints.

For an older or rare piece, the questions are immediate: Is it authentic? Has it been modified or tampered with? What is its history? Can a boutique seeing it for the first time provide a reliable answer quickly?
