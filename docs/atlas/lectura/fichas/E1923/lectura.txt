# Deep Tech Mobility Operating System

A productization unit inside OÜ JUBAP focused on applied mobility orchestration for European and global mobility challenges, built from proven operational evidence, agentic shared-mobility PoC work and current research in regime-aware adaptive systems.

Applied mobility orchestration — from proven logistics intelligence to a smart-city Mobility Operating System and agentic shared-mobility add-ons.

Core product line for solving institutional, urban and networked mobility orchestration problems.

Car Evolution as an orchestration add-on for existing carpooling and shared-mobility solutions.

The unit converts proven orchestration know-how into a startup product line addressing mobility cost, congestion, emissions, accessibility, fleet utilisation and operational resilience, while keeping research, lineage evidence and PoC work clearly separated.
