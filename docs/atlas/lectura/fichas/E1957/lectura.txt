# Car Evolution Mobility OS – Materials

Car Evolution is a Distributed Mobility Operating System / Mobility Control Center for authorities, campuses, fleets, operators and mobility ecosystems that need to coordinate existing capacity. The core product is the orchestration layer: demand and capacity modelling, policy-weighted scenarios, route and capacity planning, execution instructions, KPI tracking and affected-subspace replanning. User-facing, smart-contract, settlement and edge-agentic modules are treated as extensions around that core.
