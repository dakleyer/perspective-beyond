Logistics and Mobility

Case 03 · Tallinn old town edge · access window 07:30–09:30 · policy as a number Source: POLITICA_CHOICES +3 to N, POLITICA_TRANSBORDOS_CHOICES CT DIN FO LO NT NP, valor_politicas , valor_puntualidad , valor_ocupacion , valor_ahorro_unidades

The policy scale is not a switch. It runs from +3 to −5 , and it is asymmetric on purpose: the penalty for an option we want avoided is heavier than the reward for one we prefer. That is how a city reasons about restriction, and it was in the constants a decade ago.

Every point carries its own transfer policy, including one that is not a pickup point at all. Toompea is refused, and the refusal is a policy value rather than an omission.

Two weightings are scored side by side and the authority takes B. Punctuality falls from 0.91 to 0.78. The cost is recorded next to the choice , because the decision has to survive being asked about in public, and a decision whose price was never written down cannot.
