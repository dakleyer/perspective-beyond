A practical logistics system can calculate thousands or millions of technical possibilities while still leaving the most important decision to people: which competing operational objectives deserve priority. xSeil combined a reusable library of feasible route structures, scenario-dependent mathematical scoring, human selection of acceptable trade-offs, and iterative allocation that recalculated the consequences of each commitment. The result was an operational decision architecture connecting planning, vehicle resources, passengers and real-world execution.
