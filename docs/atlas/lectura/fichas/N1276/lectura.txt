# Tiger Team Maturity Model

The Texas-to-Mexico transfer case provides a setting in which a capability had to work under receiving conditions. A maturity assessment should examine repeatable handling of exceptions, maintained procedures and learning. Keep self-assessment, audit readiness and an external certification as different conclusions requiring their own evidence.

The maturity model distinguishes improvised expert response from a repeatable, learning and mission-critical capability. Progress depends on evidence, method, institutional memory and disciplined adaptation.

JubAp.Net has always been intense about methodology, standards and disciplined execution.

It means taking standards seriously enough to understand why they exist, then applying them from first principles to the reality of the work being performed.

The historical JubAp.Net lineage has worked with ISO 9001-style quality management discipline, and has maintained strong interest in ISO/IEC 20000 for service management and ISO/IEC 27001 for information security management. These standards matter because mission-critical engineering cannot depend only on talent, improvisation or individual heroics.
