# Tiger Teams Model and AI Integrity Management Systems

AI integrity is presented as an operational condition: the transformed system must remain trustworthy while work continues. Governance, evidence and intervention therefore remain connected to the mission boundary.

The enterprise-function proposal concerns responsibility that persists after implementation. Specify the operating boundary, the specialist authorities, the exception path and the receiving owner. A delivery team can build the mechanisms, but the handover must identify who can maintain and revise them during normal operation.

In mission-critical transformation, the project ends the moment operational integrity collapses.

This is the central principle behind the JubAp.Net approach to AI Integrity Management Systems.

Integrity is not treated as an external compliance layer, a cybersecurity checklist, an ethics policy, a governance dashboard or a post-deployment audit exercise. It is the condition that allows a transformed operation to remain trustworthy while it continues running.
