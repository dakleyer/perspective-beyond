JubAp.Net

The research and publication policy separates custody, authorship and assessment status. Record what an institutional receiver can retain, explain and make available, with the relevant rights and limits. A public contribution, an adopted method and an independently evaluated result remain separate states.

The architectural-continuity account suggests a valuable role for the circle: retain what an operating problem taught even when it is not ready for commercial generalisation. The field question is what survives the transition from a particular team and client setting into a reusable body of knowledge. Some elements may become a framework; others may remain a bounded case or an unresolved research question. That selective process preserves intellectual continuity without implying common ownership, universal usefulness or validation by association.
