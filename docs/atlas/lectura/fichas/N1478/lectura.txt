# JubAp.Net

JubAp.Net is a frontier engineering & operations firm with Nokia RC roots, built around mission-critical systems, and validated across multiple technology waves and industrial environments.

JubAp.Net operates an AI Development Center under a Skunk Works-style model building ahead-of-the-curve operational systems.

It also operates an engineering intervention capability based on its own Tiger Team model, designed for rapid deployment, high-impact interventions on complex & fragmented environements.

With a global operating structure, JubAp.Net acts as the base unit in Mexico , supported by JubAp.US as virtual Regional Deployment & Knowledge Transfer Node in San Francisco .

JubAp.Net modern technology base is built upon an older regional innovation continuity operating since 1898 in precision repair, wathmaking, optics and early oil activity.
