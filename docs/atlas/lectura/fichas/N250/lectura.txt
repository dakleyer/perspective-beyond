# Case Studies

The Frontier Engineering series follows practical systems work from architecture and integration to field deployment, operational continuity and reusable engineering capability. Its cases connect technical decisions with the constraints of the operating environment.

Read each mission through its engineering challenge, the interfaces that had to work together and the capability left in operation. The field archive retains its original cases, photographs and cross-unit lineage.
