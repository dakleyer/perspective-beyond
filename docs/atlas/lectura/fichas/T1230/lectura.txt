# Field Notes

Four research paths to measure avoidable suffering, test the limits of agentic systems and compare whether chosen solidarity can solve problems that competition, orders or negotiation cannot.

Cuatro vías de investigación para medir sufrimiento evitable, poner a prueba los límites de los sistemas agénticos y comparar si la solidaridad elegida puede resolver problemas que la competencia, la orden o la negociación no resuelven.

A concise guide to the paper’s central argument, how individually sound controls can fail together, and the role of the technical annexes.

A guide for readers and collaborators to review, challenge and contribute to the technical work.

An appendix linking the claims of the paper to the supporting evidence and the bounds of that evidence.
