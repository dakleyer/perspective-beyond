Informational friction as a systems problem — why a representation that governs a flow it cannot fully observe tends to deform that flow, under conditions this paper makes explicit. Stated for systems in general, human or not.

Document Status — Foundational Working Paper · Paper 1 · Series: Informational Friction (a systems theory)

The title and section titles are working titles. This is the anchor of the systems-theory series, which re-derives in pure systems terms — independent of whether the agents are human — the object behind three companion series: The Cost of Clarity (information theory), The Attribution Gap (economics), and Human Intelligence Debt (organizational theory). The forward forecasts (the Outlook) and the consolidated falsification battery are deferred to later papers in the series.
