# Optimization, Coherence, and the Frame

Why a system optimizing on its own map cannot see the map; why coherence — not rationality — is the mode that sees it; and why a purely maximizing agent is structurally blind to exactly the capabilities that keep a system alive.

Document Status — Working Paper 2 · Series: Informational Friction (a systems theory)

Builds on Paper 1 ( The Map and the Flow ), which established the object: a representation used as a control surface, diverging from the flow it governs, with back-action. This paper supplies the agent-level mechanism beneath that object. The forward forecasts (the Outlook) and the consolidated falsification battery are deferred to later papers in the series; falsifiable claims are marked inline.
