Why Selection Cannot See It

HomeWith your assistantClustersTagsCardsConceptsRelationshipsSources

T1491

Tegrity.AI

Insolvency, positive deviation, and a case (Nokia) in which market and evolutionary predictions pointed confidently in the wrong direction — the case that motivates the claim that the two instruments are complementary, not rival. Why selection does not refute this theory; it nests inside it.

Builds on Paper 1 ( The Map and the Flow ) and Paper 2 ( Rationality, Coherence, and the Frame ). This paper supplies the dynamics — insolvency and the closed-system destiny — and the central defense: the rival most likely to topple the theory, evolutionary / market selection, does not reach it. Nokia is the witnessed, motivating case used to show the two instruments are complementary, not rival; it is not a causal account of why any firm declined. The forward forecasts (the Outlook) and the consolidated falsification battery are deferred to later papers in the series; falsifiable claims are marked inline.
