# Research and Publication Policy

Tegrity.AI publishes applied research, engineering notes and pre-standardization materials of The Integral Management Society. This page explains the labels used on the site and how the public record is maintained.

Tegrity.AI is the issuing research initiative. Each item may also name individual authors and contributors. References to institutions, clients or technical exchanges describe the recorded relationship only and do not imply endorsement beyond the cited evidence.

AI tools may assist drafting, translation, formatting, coding, testing or editorial review. Responsibility for claims, sources and final release remains with the named author or authors and Tegrity.AI; AI assistance is not authorship or independent validation. Material funding, conflicts or relationships relevant to interpretation are disclosed in the item or its linked record.

Minor clarity, spelling and formatting corrections may be made in place and reflected in the last-updated date. A change that could affect interpretation receives a visible version note or correction notice. Withdrawn or replaced material remains identifiable with its reason and current status.

When an individual author is named: Author. (Year). Title. Document label and version. Tegrity.AI, The Integral Management Society. Canonical URL.
