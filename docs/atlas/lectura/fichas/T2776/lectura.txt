# When the Controls Work but the System Fails

This article explains the foundations and public significance of the Ecosystem Awareness work. It synthesizes constructed failure scenarios, source-based technology walkthroughs, derived requirements and a candidate architecture. The technical annexes and cited canonical repositories contain the verification record. Publication on Tegrity.AI does not imply peer review, independent validation, production readiness, adoption as a standard or endorsement by the ITU, FG-TIDA or any cited organisation.
