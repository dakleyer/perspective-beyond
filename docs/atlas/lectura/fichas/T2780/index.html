An agent may be authenticated, its action authorized and its log complete, while the combined decision is still unjustified. Six constructed cases explore that possibility across enterprise AI, urban mobility, shared false context, customer remediation, delayed software repair and rights provenance. Their common pressure is gradual or hidden contextual change that lets a locally valid result travel farther than its evidence, scope, authority or useful time permits. We derive six principles and fourteen solution-neutral requirements, connect them to scenario quality gates, challenge the principles with symbolic ablations and examine conditional transfer to other settings. A fair benchmark against ordinary and strongly defended implementations is specified, with drift as a separate stress. The result is a traceable requirements method and a concrete, falsifiable implementation hypothesis; product-level prevention is the next empirical question.
