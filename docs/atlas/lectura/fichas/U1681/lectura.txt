# Case Study: Chicontepec Megaproject

Client context: Private investors, family offices, SPVs, suppliers and operating companies entering Mexico’s Chicontepec megaproject — one of the world’s largest non-conventional oil developments.

Supporting layers: JubAp.net (Technology & Engineering) · JubAp.eu (Governance, Risk & Compliance)

The Chicontepec megaproject presented a convergence of challenges that few advisory structures are designed to handle simultaneously: experimental unconventional extraction environments, multinational operators with incompatible technologies and regulatory assumptions, Mexican public-sector procurement complexity, and significant social and environmental exposure in indigenous and rural territories.

Companies from the United States, Canada, Europe, China, Egypt and other regions entered the programme with strong technical expertise — but limited readiness for the realities on the ground. Failure in reporting, environmental controls, certification, procurement governance or community engagement could result in contract suspension, multi-million-dollar penalties or the outright collapse of the operating vehicle itself.

The field-intelligence account supplies a complementary view of the people and knowledge carried across the programme. For the operating model, name who can contract, authorise a change, accept a risk and execute the work. A legal vehicle does not automatically align all those responsibilities.
