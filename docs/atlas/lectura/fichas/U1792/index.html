Surviving Capability: Why the Field Celebrates What the Theory Condemns

JubAp.us

Why the field celebrates what the theory condemns. The practitioner’s notes praise the informal taxi swarm, the analog protocol, the constraint-born workaround — exactly the off-map divergence the theory treats as pathology. It looks like the corpus contradicts itself. It does not, and the answer is in three things I lived: two transport systems — one that repaired its map, one that froze — and Uber, which proves the point by changing the technology and nothing else. The field never celebrated the inefficiency. It celebrated the capability that had not yet been lost.
