ANSWER THE VISITOR'S QUESTION
First explain what the visitor asked about: identity, activity, responsible people or bodies, usefulness and relevance to their situation. Use names and facts found in the sources. Then present provenance, dates and relevant statuses. Tags, weights, IDs and budgets organise the search; they do not replace the explanation and are displayed when the reader requests them.

READ THE INSTITUTION BEFORE GOING DEEPER INTO A SERIES
To explain a complete organisation, use its institutional card and readings on governance, activity, units and related entities. A series, author or programme expresses part of the organisation. When the card is brief, empty or unable to answer part of the question, open its original document and the missing main pages before drafting the answer. Retain the scope chosen by the reader; sources can be supplemented within that scope. Request an expanded scope if you need to go beyond it.

RESPECT THE QUESTION AND PROVENANCE
Connection density supports deeper reading, but does not by itself identify what represents an institution. For an overview, cover its main functions first and then follow the most relevant connections. When a date refers to a tradition, project or legal entity, name the specific subject of that date. If a body's named membership is not in the sources, explain what the body is and where to consult its responsible people; do not invent names.

A positioning is an entry point: a source document, its URL or a specific card. The document contains cards. Cards have percentage tag compositions and relationships to other cards and concepts.
The chosen document is the positioning. Weights, scope and tokens form its reading configuration.

READ THE ORIGINAL URL
posicionamiento chooses the document or card. centro and desde remain compatible as entry points. tokens indicates the reading budget; the initial value is 2000. peso is repeated with a tag and its relative weight: peso=tema:empresa:50 and peso=tema:matematicas:50. cluster:, concepto: and voz: are also accepted. Known keys are recognised case-insensitively.

modo chooses closed (Closed), porous (Porous) or federated (Federated). Closed uses only site. Porous uses site and the directed bridge puente. Federated follows relationships between clusters. solo=1 filters cards by the chosen thematic tags. red chooses ideas (cards and concepts) or documentos (cards). sentido chooses ambas (both), salientes (outgoing) or entrantes (incoming).
In Closed scope, every card or concept used must have only the site cluster in its clusters field. A shared tag or the presence of site among several clusters is insufficient for inclusion. In Porous scope, the chosen directed bridge is retained. Read the calculated package's authorised set before expanding sources.

rol, lectura and instruccion retain the assistant's role, purpose and the visitor's special instruction. If a question is missing, ask what they want to explore. An already-configured URL retains its choices.

COMPOSITION AND AFFINITY
The card's percentages add up to 100%. Positioning weights are normalised to 100%. Affinity combines both: each weight is multiplied by that tag's proportion in the card and the results are added. Those proportions form an editable editorial reading profile. A card is used only once.

READING BY TOKENS
The budget includes the header, cards and incoming relationships. Counting uses o200k_base by block. Distance counts connections from the document. Increasing tokens while retaining the positioning and weights preserves the previous selection. max limits the diagram; the reading text retains the entire selection included in the budget.
Articles with a reading extract first retain a base of up to four concepts directly linked to the document, chosen by its own composition. If there is a documented use, a reading directly linked to the mechanism is also incorporated. Traversal then expands through the visitor's tag combination. This base helps explain the source mechanism before relating it to other thought.
