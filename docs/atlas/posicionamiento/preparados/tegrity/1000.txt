## Regime Awareness: Capability Development Across Four Field Cases
Document Status — Field Case · Series: Regime Awareness in Adaptive Systems , Paper 1

This article is a retrospective field-case reconstruction of engineering patterns that preceded the present Regime Awareness framework. It examines whether recurring control patterns across four operational settings can be formulated as a transferable, regime-aware decision architecture. The cases motivate that hypothesis; they do not by themselves establish domain-agnostic validity, predictive performance or causal effectiveness.
