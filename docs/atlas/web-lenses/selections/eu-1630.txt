## Case Study: HT
Changing the person so the organization can see what it could not — the perspective engine behind JUBAP.

Within the Nueva Perspectiva philosophy · Stewarded by The Integral Management Society / IMSV.org

A company changes when the people inside it can see and do what they could not before. That is why our organizational work is paired with a discipline of human transformation — and why both live inside the same philosophy: Nueva Perspectiva .

Mindfulness is often what people notice first, but it is only one instrument. Human transformation is the broader practice: transpersonal coaching, experiential work, deep personal retreats, emotional intelligence and conscious attention — chosen and combined for the situation. The aim is never a pleasant experience that fades. It is a genuine change of perspective that lets a person locate their own blockages, see new options, and act.

JUBAP is Nueva Perspectiva put to work. The framework moves in two directions — and human transformation powers one of them.

## China Tech Visibility & Rationalization
A global group operating across many entities had almost no consolidated visibility of its Chinese technology ecosystem. China was operating under its own IT conditions, with local platforms, local delivery models, specific compliance constraints, different digital channels and limited architectural transparency with the rest of the global organization.

This created two parallel technology worlds: the global enterprise architecture landscape, and the China-specific digital and application ecosystem. Both had legitimate reasons to exist. The challenge was not to force China into the global model, but to create architectural visibility, governance understanding and selective rationalization opportunities without breaking local compliance, cultural fit or operational autonomy.
