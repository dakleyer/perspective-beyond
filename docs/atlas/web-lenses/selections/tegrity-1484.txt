## The Map and The Flow
Informational friction as a systems problem — why a representation that governs a flow it cannot fully observe tends to deform that flow, under conditions this paper makes explicit. Stated for systems in general, human or not.

## Optimization, Coherence, and the Frame
Why a system optimizing on its own map cannot see the map; why coherence — not rationality — is the mode that sees it; and why a purely maximizing agent is structurally blind to exactly the capabilities that keep a system alive.

Builds on Paper 1 ( The Map and the Flow ), which established the object: a representation used as a control surface, diverging from the flow it governs, with back-action.
