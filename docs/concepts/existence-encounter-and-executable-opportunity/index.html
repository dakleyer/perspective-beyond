    Cross-frontier activation

    Cross-frontier activation needs an observed encounter and a justified capability link; a shared mathematical region alone does not supply either.

    El overlay añade condiciones a un modelo sintético de dos proveedores y cuatro observadores, no a una red real de cuatro proveedores.

    Resource arbitration

    The cartography overlay adds an encounter gate to the two-provider model; its four observers and geometric score are separate from physical resources and realized mission output.

    El enlace es interno a una rama abierta; no demuestra por sí mismo generalización a N proveedores ni a redes reales.

    Chosen solidarity needs an admissible transition with resources and consent, not merely a desirable end state or an aggregate score.
