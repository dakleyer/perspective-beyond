    Organisational attribution

    Institutional preservation can keep a capability available while leaving its contribution, recognition and legal rights as separate questions.

    La custodia institucional no prueba contribución económica ni transfiere por sí misma todos los derechos legales.

    Domain knowledge

    Frontier engineering can feed selected artifacts into institutional stewardship, with lineage, scope and readiness retained before broader reuse.

    La transferencia es selectiva. Participar en un círculo no transfiere automáticamente propiedad o derechos de todos los proyectos.

    Transmission of tacit judgment

    Preserving an artifact is only one layer; the tacit-knowledge model explains how observation, rehearsal and apprenticeship can preserve the judgment needed to use it.

    La comparación propone una forma de transmisión; no prueba que todo conocimiento preservado se haya transmitido con éxito.
