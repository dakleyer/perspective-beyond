    Effective response capacity

    Control burden should be compared among configurations that meet the same objective and evidence obligations, including the human response work they create.

    Mínimo es relativo al conjunto evaluado, no óptimo global demostrado; un sistema sin evidencia suficiente no es barato sino no cualificado.
