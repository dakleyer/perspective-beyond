    Total intervention cost

    A Tiger Team evaluation should include activation, learning and closure costs, while distinguishing necessary mission work from avoidable compensation.

    Intensidad, cansancio o coste no bastan para clasificar HID. TTC exige condición y fecha de salida, no una etiqueta indefinida.

    Contextual admissibility

    Mission activation may respond to a change in operating assumptions, while remaining distinct from a formal regime-detection result.

    No usar narrativas de urgencia para afirmar que un régimen cambió de forma demostrada ni activar por presión sin capacidad de respuesta.

    Usable human capability

    An intense intervention should end with recovery and transfer conditions that preserve the team's capability rather than treating sustained overload as proof of commitment.

    No se diagnostica atrofia clínica ni se confirma irreversibilidad a partir de un relato de cansancio.

    • The Human Side of Tiger Teams

    Institutional stewardship

    An institutional research mission can activate a bounded engineering contribution when funding, authority, source rights and evaluation responsibilities are explicit.

    No compromiso de trabajo gratuito, adopción normativa ni independencia del evaluador por pertenecer al mismo ecosistema.

    Mobilisation threshold

    A mobilisation threshold may inform Tiger Team activation, but accumulated load must be read together with mission focus, authority, available attention and a credible exit.

    Θ se identifica retrospectivamente en la nota; no es todavía un detector predictivo ni un permiso automático de intervención.
