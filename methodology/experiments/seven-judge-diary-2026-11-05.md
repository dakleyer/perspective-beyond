## Integrator observations

**Source identity creates recording work.** A node-card read and an original-source read need
different identifiers. A metadata adapter can make an identifier's type explicit while retaining
the sealed record. It cannot turn a card read into original-source evidence.

**A useful method question remains local to the declared activity.** A characterized and assessable
function is not by that fact an observed use. Review compares the located source, the question and
the effective evaluation capability. This observation does not instruct a judge to prefer A or B.

**The two boundaries are different.** Iván corrected the earlier first-B transcription: ABCD continues
through A, B and C and stops each branch at D. The restricted factor experiment examines F1–F4 only
within adopted A/B occurrences. C receives no factor exploration in this profile; it remains available
to ABCD continuation. No adopted first-B terminal was found at the correction checkpoint.

**Preservation check.** The 14 sealed files present at that checkpoint remained byte-identical after
the policy metadata change. No node or Level 1 relationship was edited by that correction.

## How comments and conversations enter this diary

Reviewers report brief method observations after sealing: what was difficult, what helped and one
concrete improvement if warranted. Their comments are attributed to the run; integrator summaries
are labelled as summaries. Only an actual bounded reconciliation exchange is called a conversation.
No simulated dialogue or consensus is produced.

Observations remain outside blind input. If an operational repair changes the method, its reason and
revision are recorded explicitly. Difficulty, agreement and vote counts do not determine a source's meaning.

## Effort accounting

The receipt counts above are measured from the submitted files. Source-read scopes are recorded by
each reviewer. No consolidated billing or model-token measure has been captured at this checkpoint;
subjective effort comments remain estimates. Time between checkpoints includes interruptions and
does not measure inference cost.
